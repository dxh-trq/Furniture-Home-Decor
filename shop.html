    Choosing living room furniture

    Start with the sofa. Measure the wall it will sit against, then your doorways and stairwell: every product page lists the smallest opening each piece fits through. Leave around 45 cm between the sofa and a coffee table, and at least 90 cm for walkways.

    Pick fabric in person if you can. Our swatches are free, and bouclé, linen and leather all look different in morning and evening light. Everything is covered by a 30-day return window and a 10-year frame warranty.
