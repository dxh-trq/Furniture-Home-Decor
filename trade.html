Trade questions

Who can join?

Anyone who specifies or buys furniture for clients as part of their business: interior designers, architects, stagers, hospitality and workplace teams, and retailers. We may ask for a portfolio or business registration.

Can I use my discount on personal orders?

Yes, on orders for your own home, up to $10,000 a year.

How do client orders work?

Order under your trade account with your client’s delivery address, or ship to our warehouse to hold for a project. Invoices come to you, never to your client, and never show retail prices.

Can I get samples of finishes and stone?

Yes. Trade members can borrow full fabric books, finish chips and stone samples for 30 days, free both ways.
