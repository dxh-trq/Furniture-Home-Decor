## Patterns worth adopting

- **Navigation:** Mega menu split by *Room* (Living, Bedroom, Dining, Office, Outdoor) and *Category* (Sofas, Tables, Lighting, Decor) — West Elm, Crate & Barrel.
- **Homepage:** Full-bleed lifestyle hero → shop by room tiles → new arrivals carousel → shoppable lookbook → USP bar (free delivery, 30-day returns, warranty) — HAY, Ferm Living.
- **Product listing:** Filters for price, material, color, dimensions, availability; swatch previews on hover; grid/list toggle — Article, Castlery.
- **Product detail:** Gallery + 360°/AR view, variant selectors (fabric, finish, size), dimensions diagram, delivery estimate, free swatch CTA, reviews with photos, "complete the look" — Burrow, Castlery, Tylko.
- **Content:** Journal/blog, lookbooks, designer stories, care guides — Muuto, Ferm Living.
- **Trust:** Warranty, sustainability, sample program, financing, showroom finder — Floyd, Crate & Barrel.
