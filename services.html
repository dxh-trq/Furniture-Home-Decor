    Good to know

    Do I have to buy anything?

    No. Design calls and showroom appointments are free with no obligation, and our stylists aren’t paid on commission.

    What should I prepare?

    Rough measurements of the room, a few photos from each corner, and anything you already own and want to keep. If you don’t have them, that’s fine too.

    How does the full room design credit work?

    You pay $199 when you book. Place an order of $1,500 or more within 6 months and we refund the full $199.

    Can I change or cancel my session?

    Yes, up to 2 hours before, with the link in your confirmation email. More help
