      Exchanges

      Want a different color or size? Choose exchange when you start a return. We deliver the new piece and collect the old one on the same visit, so you’re never without a sofa.

      Damaged or faulty

      Send photos within 7 days of delivery and we’ll send a replacement part, a technician or a new piece, free. This doesn’t use up your 30 days. Report damage

      When the money arrives

      We refund within 5 working days of collection. Card refunds can take a further 3–5 days to show, depending on your bank. Store credit is instant.

      Return questions

      Do I need the original packaging?

      No. Our collection team brings blankets and wrap. For small items sent by label, any sturdy box works.

      Is delivery refunded?

      Yes. If you return everything in an order, we refund the delivery charge too, including white glove.

      Can I return something I’ve used?

      Yes. Living with it is the point of the 30 days. We only ask that it’s not damaged beyond normal use.

      I missed the 30 days. Can I still return it?

      Talk to us. We can’t always take it back, but we can often help with a fabric change, a swap of legs, or our refurbishment program. Contact us
