    Quick answers

    For order or delivery questions

    When will my delivery arrive?

    In-stock pieces arrive in 1–2 weeks and made-to-order in 6–8. Your account shows the booked day, and we text you the day before. Track your order

    Can I change my delivery day?

    Yes, up to 48 hours before. Use the Change date button in your account or reply to your booking text. Change delivery date

    Can I change my order?

    Fabric and size can change until your piece enters the workshop, usually 3 days after ordering.

    How do returns work?

    You have 30 days from delivery. We collect it from your home for free and refund you within 5 days of collection. Returns policy

    Does it need the original packaging?

    No. Our team wraps it on collection.

    Can I exchange instead?

    Yes. Start a return and choose exchange; we deliver the new piece and collect the old one on the same visit. Start a return

    Something arrived damaged

    Sorry about that. Send us photos within 7 days of delivery and we’ll send a replacement part or a technician, free.

    What photos help?

    One of the whole piece, one close-up of the damage, and one of the label under the seat or on the back.

    Can I see fabrics first?

    Order up to six swatches free. They usually arrive in two days. Order swatches

    Will it fit through my door?

    Use the checker in our sofa guide, or send us your measurements. Check your doorway

    Can someone help me plan a room?

    Book a free 45-minute video call with a stylist. Book a design call

    Do you work with designers?

    Yes. Trade members get 15% off, a dedicated contact and samples on loan. Trade program

    Can you supply hotels and offices?

    We make contract-grade versions of most pieces with fire-rated fabrics. Tell us about the project below.
