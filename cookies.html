1What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website saves in your browser, so it can remember things between pages and visits, like what’s in your cart. We also use your browser’s local storage for a few features, like the delivery checklist, and treat it the same way.

First-party cookies are set by Morrow. Third-party cookies are set by other companies whose services we use, like our payment provider. We only work with companies that have a contract with us about how they use data. Read our privacy policy for more.

    4Other ways to control cookies

    You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Look for “Privacy” or “Cookies” in the settings of Chrome, Safari, Firefox or Edge. If you block essential cookies, you won’t be able to sign in or check out.

    Browsers that send a Global Privacy Control signal automatically turn off our advertising cookies.
