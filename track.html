What each status means

Every delivery moves through the same five steps. Pieces in stock skip straight from ordered to packed.

How delivery works

  1. Ordered

    We’ve got your order and emailed a confirmation. Most orders can still be changed for an hour.

  2. Being made

    Made-to-order pieces are built for you in our workshops. This usually takes 6 to 8 weeks.

  3. At our hub

    Your delivery is at the hub nearest you, checked for damage and ready for the day you booked.

  4. Out for delivery

    The team is on the road. You’ll get a 30-minute window by text and can follow the van here.

  5. Delivered

    Carried to your room, unwrapped and placed. Returns are free for 30 days from today.

Delivery questions

Can’t see your answer? Our help center has more.

Where do I find my order number?

It starts with MR- and is at the top of your confirmation email and on your receipt. If you have an account, it’s also under Orders.

Why is my order in more than one delivery?

Pieces that are in stock ship straight away. Made-to-order pieces follow when they’re ready, so you’re not kept waiting for everything. Every delivery is free once your order is over $500.

Can I change my delivery day?

Yes, for free, up to 48 hours before. Look up your order above and choose “Change date”. After that, call us and we’ll do what we can.

Does someone need to be home?

Yes, someone over 18 needs to be there to let the team in and check the pieces. They don’t need to be the person who ordered.

My order hasn’t moved in a while

Made-to-order pieces can sit at “Being made” for a few weeks while they’re built. If your estimated week has passed, contact us and we’ll find out where it is.

Something arrived damaged

Don’t worry, and don’t throw the packaging away yet. Take a photo and tell us within 7 days of delivery; we’ll send a replacement part or piece at no cost.
