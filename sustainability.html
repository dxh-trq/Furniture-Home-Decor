The greenest sofa is the one you never replace

Furniture is heavy, and making it has a cost. We’re not a zero-impact company, and anyone selling sofas who says they are isn’t being straight with you. Here’s what our furniture costs the planet, what we’re doing about it, and what we haven’t solved yet.

  • 100%FSC-certified oak and birch in every frame
  • 70%recycled fiber in our bouclé
  • Plastic-freepackaging on all furniture since 2024
  • 2,300pieces collected and refurbished last year

What it’s made of

Inside an Alder sofa

Fabric

70% recycled bouclé, European linen and vegetable-tanned leather. Every fabric is tested for 50,000 rubs before we use it.

Wood

Solid FSC-certified oak and walnut, and birch plywood only where it adds strength. Finished with plant-based oils, not lacquer.

Stone

Travertine offcuts from a family quarry in Tivoli, cut to size rather than quarried for us.

Metal

Recycled steel springs, and lamp bodies in recycled aluminum, spun and powder-coated without solvents.

Nothing we make should end up in landfill

Every Morrow piece has a way back to us. Last year we collected 2,300 pieces: 71% went back to new homes as seconds, 24% were stripped for parts, and 5% were recycled.

What we haven’t solved

Foam

Most seat foam is still made from oil. Plant-based foams we’ve tested so far sag within two years, and we won’t sell a sofa that does.

Stone from Italy

Travertine is heavy and travels a long way. We only use offcuts and ship by sea, but it’s still our highest-carbon product per kilo.

Leather

Our leather is a by-product of the food industry and vegetable-tanned, but cattle farming has a large footprint we can’t fix.

Delivery miles

Two-person delivery is how furniture arrives safely, but it means vans. Our electric fleet is growing slower than we planned.
