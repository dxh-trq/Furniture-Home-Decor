Gift card questions

Do gift cards expire?

No. Morrow gift cards never expire, and there are no fees to use or hold them.

Can I use more than one gift card on an order?

Yes, up to five, and you can pay any remaining amount by card, Apple Pay, PayPal or Klarna.

What if they return something bought with a gift card?

The amount goes back onto a gift card, so it’s still theirs to spend.

I sent an e-gift to the wrong email. What now?

Contact us with your order number. If it hasn’t been used yet, we’ll cancel it and send a new one.

Can I buy a gift card in a showroom?

Yes. Showroom cards come in the same linen envelope, and work online too.
