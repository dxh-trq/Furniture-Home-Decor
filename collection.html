Nordic calm

Pale oak, white bouclé and soft sage. Light pieces that make a small room feel bigger, and a big room feel calm.

  • Pale oak
  • White bouclé
  • Sage linen
  • Brushed steel
Shop 38 pieces Buy the set, save 10%

The story

We asked what a room needs to feel calm at the end of a long day. The answer was less: pale wood that lets the light in, fabrics you want to touch, and nothing that shouts.

“Every piece had to earn its place. If it didn’t make the room lighter, it didn’t make the cut.”Signe Holm, designer

  • Light woods

    Pale oak and ash, oiled not lacquered, so the grain stays soft.

  • Soft edges

    Rounded arms and corners, safer for small homes and easy on the eye.

  • Room to breathe

    Raised legs and open bases, so floors show and rooms feel larger.

Warm minimal

The story

Warm minimal started with one walnut sideboard and a question: what if you owned fewer things, but loved every one of them?

“Minimal doesn’t have to mean cold. Dark wood, leather and a warm light do most of the work.”Theo Marsh, designer

  • Rich materials

    Solid walnut, vegetable-tanned leather and unlacquered brass that age well.

  • Low and grounded

    Deep seats and low tables that make a room feel settled.

  • Warm light

    Lamps and pendants with warm white bulbs and fabric or brass shades.
