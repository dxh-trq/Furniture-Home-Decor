    Delivery questions

    Can I change my delivery day?

    Yes, for free, up to 48 hours before. Go to your orders or call us. Within 48 hours we’ll still try, but can’t always promise a new day.

    What if I’m not home?

    Someone over 18 needs to be there to let the team in. If nobody answers, we’ll call and wait 15 minutes. A second attempt costs $49.

    What if it doesn’t fit through the door?

    Our teams take off legs and doors to get most pieces in. If it truly won’t fit, we take it back and refund you in full, delivery included.

    Do you deliver to Alaska, Hawaii or outside the US?

    We deliver to Alaska and Hawaii on request, with a quote based on size. We don’t deliver outside the US yet.

    Something arrived damaged. What now?

    Tell the team before they leave if you can, or send photos within 7 days. We’ll send a part, a technician or a new piece, free. Returns and damage
