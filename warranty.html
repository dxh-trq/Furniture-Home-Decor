    Covered

    Defects in materials or in the way we made it, in normal home use.

    • Frames that crack, split or come loose at the joints
    • Springs and webbing that break or lose their tension
    • Seams that open and zips that fail in normal use
    • Foam that loses more than 15% of its height
    • Finishes that peel, bubble or crack
    • Drawer runners, hinges and wiring that stop working

    Not covered

    Things that happen to well-loved furniture. We can still help, for a fee.

    • Normal wear, like softening seats and fabric pilling
    • Stains, spills and fading from direct sunlight
    • Scratches and damage from pets, knocks or moving house
    • Pieces used outdoors or in commercial spaces
    • Changes or repairs made by someone other than us
    • Natural variation in wood grain, stone and leather

      Warranty questions

      Do I need to register my furniture?

      No. Your warranty starts on your delivery date and is linked to your order automatically.

      Does the warranty move with the furniture if I sell it?

      Yes. The warranty belongs to the piece, not the buyer. The new owner just needs the original order number.

      What about pieces I bought as seconds or ex-display?

      They have the full 10-year frame warranty. Fabric and finish cover is 1 year, as they’ve already been used.

      Is the warranty in addition to my legal rights?

      Yes. It doesn’t affect your rights under consumer law, and we’ll always honour whichever gives you more.

      Can I read the full terms?

      Yes. They’re in our terms of sale, in plain English.
