  • You’re in controlChange your choices below, or ask for a copy of your data or to delete it.
  • Not kept foreverWe delete what we no longer need, on the schedule in section 6.

1Who we are

Morrow Home, Inc. (“Morrow”, “we”, “us”) makes and sells furniture and home decor online, in our showrooms and through our trade program. We are responsible for the personal information described in this policy. Our registered office is 210 Kent Avenue, Brooklyn, NY 11249.

This policy covers our website, showrooms, delivery and customer service. It doesn’t cover other companies’ websites that we link to.

2What we collect

We collect information you give us, like your address when you order, and a small amount automatically when you use our website. Here’s all of it:

3How we use it

  • To fulfil your order: making, delivering and supporting what you buy, including returns and warranty claims.
  • To talk to you: order updates, answers to your questions and, if you’ve agreed, marketing emails.
  • To improve Morrow: understanding which pages help and which confuse, if you allow analytics.
  • To keep things safe: preventing fraud and protecting our customers, staff and systems.
  • Because the law says so: keeping tax records and responding to lawful requests.

4Who we share it with

We never sell your personal information. We share it only with companies that help us run Morrow, and only what they need.

Every provider has a contract with us that stops them using your information for anything else. Under some US state laws, letting advertising partners use cookie IDs can count as “selling” or “sharing” even though no money changes hands. You can turn it off in your privacy choices.

5Cookies

Cookies are small files that help the website remember things, like what’s in your cart. We only use analytics and advertising cookies if you allow them. Our cookie policy lists every cookie we use.

6How long we keep it

We keep information only as long as we need it, as set out in the table in section 2, and then delete or anonymise it. Order and payment records are kept for 7 years because tax law requires it. If you delete your account, we delete your account information within 30 days.

7Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • See itGet a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct itFix anything that’s wrong or out of date.
  • Delete itRemove your information, except what we must keep by law.
  • Take it with youReceive your information in a file you can use elsewhere.
  • Opt outStop marketing, personalisation and advertising sharing.
  • Be treated fairlyWe won’t charge you more or treat you differently for using these rights.

Residents of California, Colorado, Connecticut, Oregon, Texas, Virginia and other states with privacy laws have these rights by law. You can also ask an authorized agent to make a request for you. If we turn down a request, you can appeal by replying to our decision.

8Make a request

What would you like to do?

Check your email

9Children

Morrow isn’t for children under 16, and we don’t knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

10Keeping it safe

We encrypt information when it’s sent and stored, limit who on our team can see it, and test our systems regularly. No system is perfectly secure, so if something goes wrong that affects you, we’ll tell you quickly and explain what we’re doing about it.
