What we ask of every workshop

We only work with places we’ve been to. These are the rules, and we check them in person.

  • Paid above a living wageMeasured against the local living wage, not the minimum wage.
  • Visited at least twice a yearBy our own team, unannounced at least once.
  • Materials we can traceCertified wood, recycled metals and fibres, named quarries.
  • Fair termsWe pay in 30 days and agree prices a year ahead.
