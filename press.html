Latest news

  • 24 September 2026Showrooms Morrow opens its sixth showroom, in Los Angeles Our first West Coast showroom outside Portland opens on 14 November 2026 in the Arts District, at 800 E 3rd Street, with the full Nordic calm and Warm minimal collections, a fabric library and free design appointments.

    The 4,000 sq ft space is fitted out with furniture from our own workshops and reclaimed oak from a Hickory barn.

    Like every Morrow showroom, there are no sales targets: stylists help people sit, touch and choose, and orders are placed online.

    Media contact: press@morrowhome.com

  • 9 September 2026Product The Hale lounge chair joins Nordic calm A light lounge chair in recycled bouclé on a blackened oak frame, made in our Hickory workshop and priced at $640.

    At 14 kg, it’s the lightest upholstered piece we make, designed for small rooms and corners that need a second seat.

    Like every Morrow chair, the frame has a 10-year warranty and the cover can be replaced.

    Media contact: press@morrowhome.com

  • 18 June 2026Sustainability 2,300 pieces collected for a second life Our returns and take-back service collected 2,300 pieces last year: 71% went back to new homes as workshop seconds, 24% were stripped for parts and 5% were recycled.

    Pieces are inspected, repaired and re-covered in Hickory, then sold with the full warranty at up to 30% off.

    The program now covers every piece we make, including lighting and rugs.

    Media contact: press@morrowhome.com

  • 2 April 2026Sustainability Morrow sets its 2030 targets By 2030, every piece will use FSC-certified or reclaimed wood and at least 70% recycled or natural fibers, and every delivery will be electric.

    We will publish progress against each target every year, including the ones we miss.

    Media contact: press@morrowhome.com

  • 15 January 2026Trade Trade program opens to hospitality projects Hotels, restaurants and offices can now order Morrow furniture with commercial-grade fabrics, trade pricing and a dedicated project team.

    The first projects include a 42-room hotel in Hudson, New York, and an 84-cover restaurant in Austin, Texas.

    Media contact: press@morrowhome.com
