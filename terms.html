1About these terms

In short: These are the rules for buying from us. By placing an order, you agree to them.

These terms apply when you buy from Morrow Home, Inc. (“Morrow”, “we”, “us”) on our website, by phone or in our showrooms. They sit alongside our privacy policy, which explains how we use your information.

They’re written in plain English on purpose. If anything is unclear, ask us before you order. Nothing in these terms affects your rights under consumer protection law.

2Your account

In short: You don’t need an account to order. If you make one, keep your password to yourself.

You can check out as a guest. An account lets you track deliveries, change delivery dates and keep a wishlist. You must be 18 or over to open an account or place an order.

You’re responsible for keeping your password secure and for orders placed from your account. Tell us straight away if you think someone else has used it.

3Prices and payment

In short: Prices are in US dollars. Sales tax is added at checkout, and we take payment when you order.

All prices are in US dollars and don’t include sales tax, which is added at checkout based on your delivery address. Delivery charges are shown before you pay.

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, PayPal and Klarna. We take full payment when you order, including for made-to-order pieces. If you pay with Klarna, their own terms also apply.

If we’ve made an obvious pricing mistake, like a sofa listed at $19, we’ll contact you and you can choose to pay the correct price or cancel for a full refund.

Price protection: if the price of something you bought drops within 14 days of your order, contact us and we’ll refund the difference.

4Orders and made-to-order pieces

In short: Your order is confirmed when we email you. Made-to-order pieces take 6–8 weeks and are built just for you.

When you place an order, we email you to confirm we’ve received it. The contract between us starts when we send that email. If we can’t accept your order, for example because something has sold out, we’ll tell you and refund you in full.

Many sofas and chairs are made to order in our workshop in Hickory, North Carolina. Delivery estimates are shown on each product page and in your confirmation. They are estimates: if we’re going to be more than two weeks late, we’ll tell you, and you can cancel for a full refund.

Natural materials vary. Wood grain, stone veining and leather markings differ from piece to piece, and fabric colours can look slightly different on screen. That’s why we offer free swatches.

5Changing or cancelling an order

In short: Change or cancel for free until your piece goes into production, usually 3 days after ordering.

You can change or cancel your order for free until it goes into production. For made-to-order furniture that’s usually 3 days after you order; for in-stock pieces, it’s until we book your delivery. Go to your orders or contact us.

After that, you can still return most pieces within 30 days of delivery, as set out in section 7.

6Delivery

In short: Free delivery over $500, to the room you choose. You pick the day, and someone over 18 needs to be home.

We deliver to the 48 contiguous US states. Alaska and Hawaii are available on request. Delivery is free on orders over $500 and $49 below that. White glove delivery, with assembly and packaging removal, is $79. The full details are on our delivery page.

You choose a delivery day and time window at checkout, and can change it for free up to 48 hours before. Someone over 18 needs to be home. If nobody is there, we’ll call and wait 15 minutes; a second attempt costs $49.

Please measure your doorways, stairs and hallways before you order. If a piece won’t fit, we’ll take it back and refund you in full, including delivery.

The furniture is yours, and your responsibility, once it’s delivered.

7Returns and refunds

In short: 30 days to return almost anything, collected free. Refunds within 5 working days of collection.

You have 30 days from delivery to return most items for a full refund. We collect furniture from your home for free, and you don’t need the original packaging. Small items can go back with a free label or to any showroom. Start a return on our returns page.

These can’t be returned unless they’re faulty: custom sizes, pieces in customer’s own material (COM) and items marked final sale.

We refund to your original payment method within 5 working days of collecting everything, including delivery if you return a whole order. You can choose store credit instead, with 10% added. Items should be in the condition you’d expect after normal use; if something is damaged beyond that, we may reduce the refund and will explain why.

8Warranty and faulty items

In short: 10 years on frames and springs, 2 years on fabric and finishes. Report damage on delivery within 7 days.

Our warranty covers defects in materials and making: 10 years on frames, springs and solid wood construction, and 2 years on fabric, leather, foam, finishes and lighting. It doesn’t cover normal wear, stains, pet damage or pieces used outdoors or commercially. The details are on our warranty page.

If something arrives damaged, tell the delivery team or send us photos within 7 days. We’ll send a part, a technician or a replacement, free.

The warranty is in addition to your legal rights, not instead of them.

9Gift cards and promotions

In short: Gift cards never expire. Promo codes can’t be combined unless we say so.

Gift cards never expire and have no fees. They can’t be exchanged for cash, except where the law requires it. If you return something bought with a gift card, the refund goes back onto a gift card. Treat gift cards like cash: we can’t replace them if they’re lost or stolen once used.

Promo codes have their own conditions, shown where we offer them. Only one code can be used per order, and codes can’t be used on gift cards or trade orders.

10Trade customers

In short: Trade members get separate pricing and payment terms, set out in the trade agreement.

If you buy through our trade program, your trade agreement also applies and takes priority over these terms where they differ. Some consumer rights, like the 30-day return window, may work differently for business purchases.

11Reviews and content you share

In short: Reviews must be honest and your own. We publish good and bad ones.

If you post a review, photo or comment, it must be honest, about your own experience, and not offensive or unlawful. You keep ownership of what you share, and give us permission to show it on our website and social channels, with your first name and city.

We publish every review that follows these rules, positive or negative. We never pay for reviews or edit what they say.

Our product designs, photos, illustrations, text and logo belong to Morrow or the people we work with. You can share links and save images for personal use, but not copy or sell them.

Please don’t try to break, overload or scrape our website, or use it for anything unlawful. We work hard to keep it available and accurate, but can’t promise it will always be free of errors or interruptions.
