How to choose a sofa that fits your room and your doorway

Most sofa returns have nothing to do with comfort. The sofa was too deep for the room, or it never made it past the stairs. Here’s how to avoid both.

M

Maya Okafor

Senior stylist, 18 September 2026

In this guide

  1. Measure the room
  2. Measure the way in
  3. Choose seat depth and height
  4. Pick the fabric last
  5. Before you order

Before you look at a single color, spend ten minutes with a tape measure. You need four numbers: the wall, the walkway, the narrowest doorway and the tightest turn on the way in.

1Measure the room

Measure the wall the sofa will sit against, then take off at least 30 cm so it doesn’t look squeezed in. A 3-seat sofa at 220 cm needs a wall of around 250 cm. Next, measure out from the wall: a sofa, a coffee table and a walkway need about 95 + 45 + 90 cm of floor.

Mark the footprint on the floor with painter’s tape and leave it for a day. You’ll know within an evening whether it feels right.

Stylist’s tip

Leave about 45 cm between the front of the sofa and the coffee table. Close enough to reach a cup, far enough to walk past.

2Measure the way in

This is the step people skip. Walk the route from the street to the room and measure the width of every doorway, hallway and stair turn. The number that matters is the narrowest one.

Will it fit through your door?

Enter your narrowest doorway and we’ll check it against the Alder sofa.

3Choose seat depth and height

Seat depth changes how a sofa feels more than anything else. Around 55 cm suits people who sit upright or are under 170 cm. Deeper seats, 60 cm and over, are for lounging with your legs up, and usually need a cushion behind your back if you want to sit straight.

“Sit on it the way you actually sit at home, not the way you sit in a showroom.”

Seat height matters if anyone in your home finds low seats hard to get out of. 45 cm is standard. Anything under 40 cm feels lounge-like and looks lower in a room.

4Pick the fabric last

Once you know the size and shape, fabric is easier. Bouclé hides wear and suits homes with pets that don’t scratch. Linen softens beautifully but creases. Leather is the easiest to wipe clean and gets better with age. Order swatches and look at them in morning and evening light before you decide.

Order up to six free swatches

5Before you order

  • The wall is at least 30 cm wider than the sofa
  • There’s 90 cm of walkway in front of the coffee table
  • The narrowest doorway is wider than the sofa’s height with legs off
  • Stair turns and lifts are measured, including the ceiling above the stairs
  • You’ve seen the fabric in your own light
