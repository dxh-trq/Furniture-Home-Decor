    How reviews work

    Reviews only help if you can trust them. These are our rules.

    • Only from real ordersYou can only review a piece you bought from us, and every review is marked verified.
    • We publish the bad onesWe never hide or delay a review because of its rating. About 6% of our reviews are 3 stars or lower.
    • No paid or gifted reviewsWe don’t offer discounts or prizes for reviews, and staff can’t review our products.
    • We reply to problemsOur team replies to every review of 3 stars or lower, usually within a day, and says what we did.
    • What we removeOnly personal details, offensive language, or reviews about something other than the product or service.
    • Edit any timeYou can update your review from your account, for example after a year of use.
