The Morrow apprenticeship

From beginner to upholsterer in 18 months, paid from day one

Upholstery is a skill that’s disappearing. Each year we train 8 apprentices in our Hickory workshop, with a mentor, three skills checks and a pay rise at each one. No experience needed.

From the team

Questions about working here

Do you sponsor visas?

For some studio roles, yes. The role description says if it’s possible. We can’t sponsor visas for workshop, showroom or delivery roles at the moment.

Can I apply for more than one role?

Yes, but pick the two that fit you best. It helps us get you to the right person quickly.

Do you make adjustments for interviews?

Always. Tell us what helps, like extra time, questions in advance, or a quiet room, when you apply or at any point after.

Will I hear back if I’m not successful?

Yes. Everyone who applies gets a reply, and everyone we interview gets feedback if they want it.
