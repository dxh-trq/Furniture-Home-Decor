Built to be sat on every day

We designed the Alder around how people actually use a sofa: for films, naps, guests and the occasional spilled drink. Every part can be replaced, so it can outlast the next three moves.

  • Kiln-dried oak frame

    Corner-blocked, glued and screwed. It won’t twist or creak, and it’s covered by our 10-year warranty.

  • Pocket-sprung seat

    Individually wrapped springs under a layer of recycled foam and feather. Supportive when you sit up, soft when you don’t.

  • Covers that come off

    Every cushion cover unzips and washes at 30°C. Replacement covers are sold separately if you change your mind about the color.

  • Fits through the door

    The legs unscrew and the back cushions lift off, so it fits through an 80 cm doorway. The corner version arrives in two pieces.

Care

Vacuum weekly with a soft brush attachment. Blot spills straight away with a clean, damp cloth. Covers can be washed at 30°C on a gentle cycle and line dried. Rotate and plump seat cushions every week to keep them even.

Delivery and returns

Free delivery to the room of your choice on orders over $500. In-stock colors arrive in 1–2 weeks; made-to-order colors take 6–8 weeks. We’ll text you a two-hour window the day before.

Not right? You have 30 days from delivery to return it. We collect it for free.

Assembly

Screw in the four legs by hand and place the cushions. It takes about five minutes and needs no tools. Our delivery team can do it for you at no charge.

Warranty

10 years on the frame and springs, 2 years on fabric and cushions. Register your sofa to make a claim online.
