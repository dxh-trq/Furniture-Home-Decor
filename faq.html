Orders and payment

Can I change or cancel my order?

Yes, until your piece goes into production. For made-to-order furniture that’s usually 3 days after you order; for in-stock pieces, until we book your delivery. Go to your orders or call us.

After that, you can still return it within 30 days of delivery.

Was this helpful?
Which payment methods do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, PayPal and Klarna. We take payment when you order, including for made-to-order pieces.

Was this helpful?
How does paying in 4 instalments work?

Choose Klarna at checkout. You pay a quarter today and the rest every two weeks, with no interest. Klarna runs a quick eligibility check that doesn’t affect your credit score.

Was this helpful?
The price dropped after I ordered. Can I get the difference?

Yes, if it drops within 14 days of your order. Contact us with your order number and we’ll refund the difference to your original payment method.

Was this helpful?
Do your prices include sales tax?

No. Sales tax is added at checkout based on your delivery address. Delivery is free on orders over $500.

Was this helpful?

Delivery

How long does delivery take?

In-stock pieces arrive in 1–2 weeks. Made-to-order furniture, such as sofas in some fabrics, takes 6–8 weeks. Each product page shows which applies to the color you choose.

Was this helpful?
How much is delivery?

Free on orders over $500, and $49 below that. Every delivery is carried into the room you choose by a two-person team. White glove delivery, with assembly and packaging removal, is $79.

Was this helpful?
Can I choose my delivery day?

Yes. You pick a day and a time window at checkout, and can change it up to 48 hours before in your account. We text you the day before and again when the team is 30 minutes away.

Was this helpful?
Why is my order arriving in two deliveries?

If you order in-stock and made-to-order pieces together, we send the in-stock pieces first so you’re not waiting weeks for a lamp. You only pay delivery once.

Was this helpful?
Will a sofa fit through my door?

Measure your narrowest doorway, hallway and stair turn. Our sofa legs unscrew, so most sofas fit through an 80 cm door. Use the doorway checker or send us your measurements.

Was this helpful?
Do you deliver outside the US?

Not yet. We deliver to all 48 contiguous states. Alaska and Hawaii are available on request.

Was this helpful?

Returns and refunds

What is your return policy?

You have 30 days from delivery to decide. We collect furniture from your home for free, and you don’t need the original packaging. Refunds reach you within 5 working days of collection.

Was this helpful?
How do I start a return?

Go to your orders and choose Start a return, or contact us. We’ll email you a collection date within one working day.

Was this helpful?
Can I return made-to-order furniture?

Yes, on the same 30-day terms. We refurbish returned pieces in our workshop and sell them as seconds.

Was this helpful?
Can I exchange for a different color?

Yes. Choose exchange when you start a return. We deliver the new piece and collect the old one on the same visit, so you’re never without a sofa.

Was this helpful?

Product care

Can I wash the sofa covers?

Yes. All seat and back cushion covers unzip and wash at 30°C on a gentle cycle. Line dry and put them back on while slightly damp so they keep their shape.

Was this helpful?
How do I deal with spills?

Blot, don’t rub, with a clean damp cloth as soon as possible. For bouclé, lift fibres afterwards with a soft brush. For leather, use a slightly damp cloth and let it dry naturally.

Was this helpful?
How do I care for solid oak and walnut?

Dust with a dry cloth and re-oil once or twice a year with the care kit we include. Keep pieces out of strong direct sunlight and away from radiators. Our care guides cover water rings and scratches.

Was this helpful?
Is travertine hard to look after?

It’s sealed before it leaves us. Use coasters for wine, coffee and citrus, and wipe spills quickly. Reseal once a year with the included sealant.

Was this helpful?

Materials and sizing

Can I see fabrics before ordering?

Yes. Order up to six free swatches and they usually arrive in two days. Order swatches.

Was this helpful?
Which fabric is best with pets?

Leather wipes clean and doesn’t hold hair. Tightly woven linen is a good second choice. Bouclé loops can catch on claws, so we don’t recommend it with cats that scratch.

Was this helpful?
Is your furniture really solid wood?

Frames, legs and tables are solid oak or walnut. Some large panels, like sideboard backs and drawer bottoms, use birch plywood for stability, and every product page says exactly where.

Was this helpful?
Where can I find exact dimensions?

Every product page has a dimensions drawing and table, including seat height, seat depth and the smallest doorway the piece fits through.

Was this helpful?

Warranty and repairs

What does the warranty cover?

10 years on frames and springs, and 2 years on fabric, cushions and finishes, against defects in materials or making. It doesn’t cover normal wear, pet damage or stains.

Was this helpful?
Something arrived damaged. What now?

Sorry about that. Send us photos within 7 days of delivery through the contact form and we’ll send a replacement part or a technician, free.

Was this helpful?
Can I buy replacement parts?

Yes. Covers, cushions, legs and feet are all sold separately, even for pieces no longer in our range.

Was this helpful?

No, you can check out as a guest. An account lets you track deliveries, change dates and keep a wishlist across devices.

Use the unsubscribe link in any email, or switch them off in account settings. Delivery updates are separate and keep coming.
